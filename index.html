Privacy Policy for Japadhara

App: Japadhara (జపధార - Devotional Chant Tracker) • Effective Date: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Japadhara is a devotional chant tracker created to help spiritual practitioners record and track their sacred mantra chants. We place the highest value on your spiritual privacy and personal data. Japadhara is built on an offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture: we do not run any central servers, we do not require account creation on our own systems, and we never collect, track, monetize, or sell your personal information.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Locally Stored Data:

  • Chant Records: Timestamp, chant count, and optional notes you write.
  • App Preferences: Daily chant goal, starting baseline count, mantra text, language choice, and preset mode.

All your chant logs and preferences are stored strictly on your local device (using encrypted/private local app storage). If you choose not to connect Google Drive, no data ever leaves your phone.

3. Google Drive Integration & Scopes

Japadhara offers an optional cloud backup and synchronization feature powered by Google Drive. If you explicitly choose to connect your Google Account:

  • Scope Requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file (Per-file access).
  • Strictly Limited Access: The app only accesses files and folders that it creates itself (a folder named Japadhara). The app cannot view, read, modify, or delete any other files, photos, documents, or spreadsheets in your personal Google Drive.
  • Files Created: Japadhara creates two files in your private Japadhara folder:
    • japadhara_backup.json (A lossless snapshot for backup and multi-device restore)
    • japadhara_chants.csv (A clean spreadsheet view for opening in Google Sheets / Excel)
  • Direct Transmission: Communication occurs exclusively between your device and Google's official API servers over secure HTTPS (TLS). Your Google Drive data is never routed through or stored on any intermediary or developer server.
Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
Japadhara's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Analytics, Tracking & Third-Party Advertising

  • Japadhara contains no third-party advertisements.
  • Japadhara contains no third-party tracking scripts or user profiling SDKs.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to data brokers or advertising networks.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Data: You can delete any individual chant entry at any time in the app. Uninstalling the app completely removes all local app storage from your device.
  • Google Drive Data: You can delete the Japadhara folder and its contents directly from your Google Drive at any time, or disconnect Google Drive anytime via the app's Settings screen.

6. Children's Privacy

Japadhara does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The app is completely safe for devotees and practitioners of all ages.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy. Any updates will be reflected with a revised "Effective Date" at the top of this page.
